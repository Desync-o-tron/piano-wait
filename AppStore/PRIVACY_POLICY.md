# Privacy Policy for free MIDI player (FOSS)

**Effective Date:** November 6, 2026

**free MIDI player (FOSS)** ("the App") is an iOS and iPadOS piano practice application developed and published by General Neuroscience Inc. / Luke Knoble.

## 1. Microphone & Audio Processing (100% On-Device)

free MIDI player (FOSS) uses your device's microphone solely to detect the musical notes and chords you play on an acoustic piano or keyboard during practice sessions.

- **No Recording or Storage:** Audio from the microphone is processed in real time in device memory using digital signal processing (Fast Fourier Transform pitch detection) and is immediately discarded.
- **No Transmission:** The App never records, stores, or uploads microphone audio to any server.

## 2. Imported MIDI Files

Any Standard MIDI Files (`.mid` or `.midi`) that you import into the App from the Files app are stored locally on your device inside the App's private Documents directory so you can practice them offline. You can delete imported MIDI files at any time by swiping to delete in the library.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep all piano practice features free, the App displays advertisements in the song library and on the post-song completion screen (never during active piano practice) using **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK).

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect limited non-personal device and usage information, including:
- Device identifiers (such as Vendor ID or Advertising ID where permitted)
- Coarse IP-based location (country/region)
- Ad impression, interaction, and diagnostic performance metrics

For more information on how Google uses data from partner apps, please visit [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or require account registration.
